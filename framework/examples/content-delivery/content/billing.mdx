Use this guide to manage payments for your workspace.

## Find an invoice

Open **Settings → Billing → Invoices** and choose the payment date.

## Request a refund <Anchor id="refunds" />

<Snippet id="shared.refund-window" />

The workspace owner can open **Settings → Billing → Contact support** and provide the invoice number. Support confirms eligibility before issuing a refund.

[Read the billing overview](doc:guide.billing).
