## Review a bill

Open the billing page and choose the billing period. Check the total and download
the statement before contacting support about a charge.

<ForFacet audience="internal">

## Check the retry queue

Before replaying a failed billing job, check whether the original request
completed. Record the job identifier and use the team's approved replay procedure.

</ForFacet>

## Get help

Include the statement identifier and billing period in your support request.
