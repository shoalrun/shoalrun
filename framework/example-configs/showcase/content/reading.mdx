A dispatch groups parcels collected during one pickup window. Check the destination and service before printing a manifest. The examples on this page illustrate documentation components; they are not additional API operations.

## Before you begin

<Callout type="note">All parcel IDs on this site are fictional.</Callout>
<Callout type="tip">Group parcels by pickup window to make the handoff easier to check.</Callout>
<Callout type="important">Keep the dispatch receipt until every parcel is reconciled.</Callout>
<Callout type="warning">Changing a destination after printing a label can produce a mismatch.</Callout>
<Callout type="caution">Do not put real addresses or credentials in this public example.</Callout>

## Prepare the handoff

- Check that each parcel has a readable label and that the destination matches the manifest. If a label is damaged, replace it before moving the parcel into the collection area.
- Count the parcels against the dispatch record. Investigate missing or extra parcels before confirming the handoff, and record the reason for any correction.
- Keep the receipt with the dispatch record so the operations team can trace a discrepancy without relying on a conversation or a temporary browser session.
