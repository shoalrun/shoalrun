Use this fictional checklist to review a dispatch before handing it to a courier.

## Review the manifest

<Steps headingLevel="3">
<Step title="Count the parcels" icon="package">

Compare the manifest with the parcels in the collection area.

</Step>
<Step title="Review exceptions" icon="clipboard-check">

Resolve missing labels and destination mismatches before continuing.

</Step>
<Step title="Save the receipt" icon="archive">

Keep the receipt alongside the reviewed manifest.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Resolve an exception

<CollapsibleGroup>


<Collapsible title="A parcel is missing">

Leave the handoff incomplete until the operator records an explanation and updates the manifest.

</Collapsible>
<Collapsible title="The destination changed">

Replace the label and compare it with the revised manifest before resuming the review.

</Collapsible>


</CollapsibleGroup>
