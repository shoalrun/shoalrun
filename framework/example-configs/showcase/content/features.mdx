## Inspect the second output

Use **Read as Markdown** on a page to compare its text representation. Tabs become labeled sections, diagrams retain their source and explanation, and reader-specific content keeps its role labels. Presentation controls are omitted when they have no text-only action.

The compact index at [llms.min.txt](/llms.min.txt) provides stable page IDs, descriptions, types, and token counts. The [document manifest](/documents.json) records representation digests and sizes. Search and the page outline help readers navigate the HTML.

## Customize this project

Replace the fictional service, hostnames, API source, and examples. Keep stable page IDs when changing titles or routes. Social cards and a favicon are generated from the site's theme and content.

The site builds without provider credentials. Hosted chat, embeddings, analytics, remote search, and deployment require your own services and are intentionally not connected to a demonstration account. The bundled notifications example additionally demonstrates ingredient-based recipe composition; trusted custom-component examples live in the package's examples directory.
