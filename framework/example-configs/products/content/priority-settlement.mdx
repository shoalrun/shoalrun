Priority settlement is included in Customer A's feature grants. This entire page is absent from an edition without that grant.

## Prepare the batch

1. Select payments that have completed authorization and meet the settlement rules.
2. Confirm the settlement account, currency, and batch total.
3. Assign a unique batch reference and submit the batch once.

## Check the result

Save the batch identifier and compare its accepted count with the submitted count. Investigate rejected entries before retrying them; do not resubmit payments already accepted into the batch.
