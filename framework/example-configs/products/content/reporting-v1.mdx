Reporting v1 organizes completed transactions by reporting period. Both customer editions use this release, even though they use different Payments versions.

## Prepare a daily report

1. Select a completed reporting period.
2. Choose the account and settlement currency.
3. Generate the report and record its period and creation time.

| Field | Check before reconciliation |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting period | Covers the day being reconciled. |
| Currency | Matches the settlement account. |
| Order reference | Identifies the original order. |

## Compare totals

Compare the report total to the settlement record. Investigate missing orders and currency mismatches before closing the period.
