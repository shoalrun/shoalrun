Run this checklist before closing a reporting period. The same source page serves Payments v2, Payments v3, and Reporting v1; each publication keeps its own links and navigation.

## Before you start

- Confirm the product and release shown in the page header.
- Check the account and currency used for the day's activity.
- Keep a list of orders that still need investigation.

## Close the day

1. Review completed payments and unsettled orders.
2. Compare the settlement total with the matching reporting period.
3. Record any unresolved discrepancy and assign an owner.
